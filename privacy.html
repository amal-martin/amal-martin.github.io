Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This page explains what personal data is processed when you visit this portfolio site, why, and what rights you have over it.

Data controller

Amal Martin, an independent UI/UX designer based in Bengaluru, India. The simplest way to reach me is by email at amalmartin0075@gmail.com.

What data is processed, and why

Contact

This site has no contact form and runs no server of its own. The “Email” and “Work” links on the Contact section are plain mailto: links — they open your own email client, addressed to amalmartin0075@gmail.com or amal.m@kristujayanti.com. Nothing is captured by this site when you click one. Whatever you choose to send arrives as a normal email, stored in my Gmail inbox, and the lawful basis for that processing is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable) — you initiated contact, and only what you send is collected.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / GitHub B.V.). Loading any page sends standard request metadata — IP address, timestamp, user agent, requested path — to GitHub's servers, which is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for how they handle this.

Fonts and styling

Typefaces are not loaded from any external service — this site uses the system font already installed on your device (Helvetica Neue, or the closest equivalent), so no font files are requested beyond this page itself. Page styling is loaded from the Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com), which receives standard request metadata such as your IP address as part of normal request handling.

Analytics & cookies

This site does not use any analytics tool, tracking pixel, advertising script, or cookie. No visitor behaviour is logged, measured, or stored by me in any form.

International transfers

GitHub and Tailwind Labs are US-based companies. Where personal data such as your IP address is transferred outside your jurisdiction as part of standard request metadata, these providers rely on their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses and/or participation in applicable data-transfer frameworks, as described in their respective privacy policies.

Retention

This site does not collect or store any personal data itself, so there is nothing retained on my end beyond what's described above. An email you choose to send me is kept for as long as useful to our conversation and any reasonable follow-up, and no longer than 24 months from the last interaction — unless a legal obligation requires longer. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, India, or another jurisdiction with similar protections, you may have rights such as:

  • access to the data I hold about you;
  • correction of inaccurate data;
  • erasure (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restriction of, or objection to, processing;
  • receiving your data in a portable format;
  • lodging a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Since this site itself doesn't collect personal data, these rights apply mainly to any email you send me directly. To exercise any of them, email amalmartin0075@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the current version.
