Fitness Centre

A gym management platform: RFID check-in, member tracking, and a trainer portal for live gym activity, built to run any fitness centre.

Role
UI/UX Design

The brief

Most gyms track membership on a spreadsheet and attendance on a sign-in sheet, with no link between the two. Fitness Centre needed to close that gap: RFID check-in and check-out at the door, a member view of their own streaks and session history, and a trainer view of who is on the floor right now, all reading from the same record. Kristu Jayanti’s own gym came on as the first deployment; the branding here is theirs, but the platform is built to run any fitness centre, not just a university’s own.

The pitch, in three cards

The landing page pairs a login by OTP, no password to forget at the gym door, with three cards that say exactly what the system does: smart membership, RFID check-in, and attendance tracking. No feature list to scroll through first.

Progress you can feel, not just log

A member’s dashboard leads with the streak, the number that actually keeps someone coming back, then backs it up with total visits, this month’s visits, and average session length. A dark, high-contrast palette with a single accent green carries through the whole member experience.

The door is the whole interface

Tapping an RFID card at the door is the entire interaction, so the screen it triggers gets to be a moment rather than a form: a name, a status, a time, and a line that reads more like a coach than a system log.

Who is actually on the floor

A trainer needs a different answer than a member does: not “how am I doing” but “who is here right now.” The trainer portal leads with live gym activity, a table of who checked in when and for how long, with a manual check-in and check-out panel for the RFID reader missing a tap.
