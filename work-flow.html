Flow

A campus commerce platform: point of sale, inventory, and staff management for campus stores, with a companion ordering app for students.

Role
UI/UX Design

The brief

Campus stores, the bookshop, the cafeteria, the stationery counter, tend to run on separate, ad hoc systems: paper receipts, manual stock counts, no shared view across locations. Flow needed to give shopkeepers one platform to run point of sale, track inventory, and manage staff, and give students a way to browse and pay for pickup without standing in line. Kristu Jayanti’s own campus stores came on as the first deployment; the stores and branding in these screens are theirs, but the platform underneath is built to run any campus’s shops.

The pitch before the login

The landing page leads with “Campus Commerce, made simple,” then gets specific fast: point of sale, inventory, financials, and staff management, each named right under the fold so a shopkeeper knows in five seconds whether this is for them.

Every store, one number

The dashboard leads with a financial overview across every campus store a shopkeeper runs, not just one: total revenue, net profit, and transaction count up top, a revenue chart by day, and a live feed of recent transactions from every location.

Built for the counter, not the back office

Point of Sale is the screen a shopkeeper’s staff open a hundred times a day, so it stays minimal: search or scan a barcode, add the item, done. No menus to dig through mid-queue.

The student side of the counter

Students browse and build a cart the same way they would on any shopping app, then pay and get a receipt with a pickup QR code, no queueing at the counter required. The shopkeeper scans the code to hand off the order.

Even the receipt is configurable

Every store’s printed receipt is a little different: different header, different footer, different fields, depending on what the shopkeeper needs. The receipt designer builds that layout section by section, with a live preview of the actual printed slip.
