Wellbeing Centre

A counselling booking platform for institutions, connecting students, counsellors, and admins.

Role
UI/UX Design

The brief

Campus counselling usually runs on manual booking, paper schedules, and no visibility into who is available when. Wellbeing Centre needed to give students a simple way to find and book a counsellor, give counsellors control over their own availability and session load, and give admins oversight of the whole program: who is using it, who is booked, and who is cleared to take sessions. Kristu Jayanti’s own wellbeing centre came on as the first deployment; the name and branding here are theirs, but the platform is built to run any institution’s counselling program under its own name.

One glance, one next step

A student’s dashboard leads with what’s next, a confirmed session, its time, its counsellor, then shows sessions completed and what’s coming up, before offering the one action that matters most: Book Session.

Choosing who to talk to

Booking starts with the counsellor, not the calendar. Each one lists their specialty (anxiety, PTSD, mindfulness, child and adolescent) right on the card, so a student can choose based on fit before they ever look at a time slot.

A private record, kept simple

Every past session lands on one timeline, scheduled, completed, or cancelled, each with its own note where one exists. The history view doesn’t try to be more than what it is: a record a student can trust and return to.

A different job, a different dashboard

A counsellor doesn’t need a booking button, they need to know who’s next. Their dashboard leads with today’s session count and the next student on the schedule, contact details and room number included, so walking into the next session takes no digging.

Setting the hours once

Counsellors set their availability as a recurring schedule, days, slot duration, break time, valid date range, instead of adding one slot at a time. The system turns that into bookable hours automatically for as long as the schedule holds.

Running the whole program

An admin’s view sits above all of it: every student, counsellor, and staff account in one table, with role, bookable status, and account status editable from the same row. Onboarding a new counsellor is a toggle, not a support ticket.
